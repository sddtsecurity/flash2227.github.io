About SDDT Security

SDDT Security (Shadow & Dark Digital Theft) is a cybersecurity initiative focused on exposing hidden digital threats and educating individuals, businesses and organisations on practical cyber defence.

Our Services

Cyber Awareness

Helping users identify online scams, phishing attacks and digital fraud.

Wi-Fi Security

Protecting wireless networks from unauthorised access.

Malware Protection

Preventing malware, spyware and ransomware attacks.

Digital Risk Assessment

Finding weaknesses before cybercriminals do.

Cyber Consulting

Professional cybersecurity advice for organisations.

Security Awareness Training

Training staff and individuals to stay safe online.
